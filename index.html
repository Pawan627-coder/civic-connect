How Civic Connect Works

Taking action against dangerous road hazards shouldn't be complicated. Here is how your report turns into real repair work.

01

Report a Problem

Notice a dangerous pothole, overflowing drain, or missing road sign? Take a photo, confirm your location on the map, and add a brief description.

✓ Geolocation tagging
02

Track Progress

Your ticket is automatically routed to the responsible civic ward office. You can track status changes from inspection to work order issuance in real time.

✓ Ward officer assignment
03

Get It Resolved

Maintenance crews perform repairs and post verified completion photos. The ticket is marked resolved and the community enjoys safer transit.

✓ Verified completion proof

Our Civic Mission

Built by Citizens, for Citizens

Civic Connect was founded on a simple belief: safe, well-maintained roads are a fundamental public right. Too often, road damage goes unaddressed because residents do not know which department to contact, or complaints get lost in administrative queues.

Our civic-tech platform brings transparency, neighborhood collaboration, and direct municipal accountability into one unified place. By documenting problems publicly and tracking solutions openly, we build stronger, safer, and more connected neighborhoods.
